# ShopSwift – E-Commerce Platform Enhancement
## Business Analysis Plan (Week 1)

A fictional, portfolio-ready business analysis case study demonstrating Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring for an e-commerce platform enhancement initiative.

> **Important:** ShopSwift is fictional. All company details, stakeholder roles, problem statements, targets, schedules and requirements are illustrative assumptions. No real interviews, customer research, production data analysis, approvals or implementation have taken place.

## Business scenario
ShopSwift is a B2C online retailer. The case study explores potential improvements to:
- Product search and filtering
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Inventory visibility
- Order tracking
- Customer complaint handling

## Project objective
Define a validated and prioritized set of business and solution requirements that could improve customer experience and operational efficiency.

## Deliverables
1. **Business Analysis Plan (DOCX):** business context, objectives, scope, approach, stakeholder analysis, BA activities, requirements management, communication, governance, monitoring, risks, timeline and artifact register.
2. **Analysis Registers (XLSX):** stakeholder register, requirements traceability matrix, risk register, communication plan and change-log template.
3. **Process model source (Mermaid):** conceptual future-state customer journey.

## How to review the project
1. Start with the Executive Summary in the DOCX.
2. Read Sections 3–5 to understand the problem, objectives and scope.
3. Review Sections 7–12 to understand stakeholder engagement, requirements and BA monitoring.
4. Open the XLSX and review each worksheet.
5. Review the process flow and explain how each step maps to the requirements.

## Key Business Analysis concepts demonstrated
- Business need and problem statement
- SMART objectives and KPIs
- In-scope / out-of-scope definition
- Stakeholder register and RACI
- Power-interest analysis
- Elicitation and analysis techniques
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- MoSCoW prioritization
- Requirements traceability
- Communication and governance
- Risk, issue and change management
- Monitoring and performance reporting

## Suggested presentation flow
1. Introduce ShopSwift and the business scenario.
2. Explain the five problem areas.
3. Present the objectives and sample KPIs.
4. Explain scope and key stakeholders.
5. Walk through the BA approach and elicitation techniques.
6. Explain one functional requirement and its acceptance criteria.
7. Show the communication plan and RACI.
8. Explain how progress, risks and changes are monitored.
9. Close with the expected benefits and next steps.

## Limitations and next steps
This is a planning case study, not a live implementation. In a real project, validate assumptions through stakeholder interviews, customer research, current-state observation, operational data, technical feasibility review, security/privacy review and formal approvals. Establish baseline KPI values before confirming targets.
